Find a Spoon shows one huge thing for a child and a grandparent to look for together. The kitchen starts with a wooden spoon. The living room and the bedroom follow.

Tap Found it when you see it. Tap Not here when that thing is not in the room. The hunt stays on this phone when you leave the app.

Find a Spoon does not use an account, ads, a camera, or a network connection. The things in the app are original.
