Find a Spoon support

Find a Spoon is a free offline hunt for kids, families, and seniors. Open the app, read the huge line, and look for that one thing in the room. Tap Found it when you see it, or Not here when it is not in the room.

The hunt stays on the phone. There is no account and no sign-in. Start a new hunt from the New hunt button when you want to begin in the kitchen again.
